# Background assets — atmosphere catalog

Source folder: `frontend/assets/background/`  
All pieces are Lemon Sky Studios / Blizzard *StarCraft: Remastered* marketing art unless noted. Most stills include Blizzard + Lemon Sky corner marks and a centered *StarCraft Remastered* logo — crop or mask those if used as live app backdrops.

**UI tip (shared):** Prefer dark voids, shadowed ground, or space fields for overlays. Bright beams, lava limbs, and logo bands need a blacked plate or heavy scrim for readable text.
